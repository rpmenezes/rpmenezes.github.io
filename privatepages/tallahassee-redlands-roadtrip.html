Houston → El Paso, TX
~750 miles · ~10 hrs · I-10 West

A big Texas day. Stopped for lunch at Casa Amaya Pupuseria & Taqueria in Boerne before pushing through the vast west Texas desert. Ended the night with dinner at L & J Cafe and beers at Old Sheepdog Brewery in El Paso.

Day 3
Friday, May 15
El Paso → Sedona, AZ
~450 miles · ~6.5 hrs · I-10 → I-25 → AZ-89A

A stunning drive through Arizona. Stopped for lunch at Guayo's El Rey in Miami, AZ on the way. The red rock formations appeared suddenly and dramatically approaching Sedona. Ended the night with dinner at Belfry Brewery in Cottonwood.

Departure El Paso — morning
Lunch Stop — Miami, AZ
  • Guayo's El Rey — Miami, AZ
Scenic Drive into Sedona — AZ-89A
  • AZ-89A from Prescott — take the scenic route north from Prescott through Jerome, a former mining town perched on a cliff. Windows down, this stretch is extraordinary.
  • AZ-89A from Flagstaff — alternatively, enter Sedona from the north through Oak Creek Canyon — equally dramatic, famous for fall colours and swimming holes.
Arrival Sedona, AZ — afternoon
Overnight — Confirmed
  • Lux Verde Hotel — 301 W. SR 89-A, Cottonwood, AZ 86326
Day 4
Saturday, May 16 — Full explore day
Sedona, Arizona
~75°F in May · Perfect hiking weather · No driving

An unforgettable day in Sedona. Started the morning with Devil's Bridge and spent the afternoon on the Cathedral Rock Trail. The views were stunning throughout. Lunch was at Hideaway House.

Morning — Devil's Bridge Trail
  • Devil's Bridge — hiked the 4-mile roundtrip trail to Sedona's iconic natural sandstone arch.
Lunch
  • Hideaway House — Sedona, AZ
Afternoon — Cathedral Rock Trail
  • Cathedral Rock Trail — short but steep hike with perhaps the most iconic view in all of Sedona. The views were stunning.
Overnight — Confirmed
  • Lux Verde Hotel — 301 W. SR 89-A, Cottonwood, AZ 86326
Dinner — Cottonwood / Sedona
  • Belfry Brewery — Cottonwood, AZ
Day 5
Sunday, May 17 — Arrival
Sedona → Redlands, California
~370 miles · ~5.5 hrs · I-40 West → I-15 → I-10

The final leg, through the Mojave Desert and into Southern California. A relaxed morning — take your time with breakfast and enjoy one last Sedona morning before heading west. Arrive in Redlands in the early to mid-afternoon.

Departure Lux Verde Hotel (Cottonwood) — use your $20 breakfast credit before checking out by 11:00 AM. Depart ~9:00–10:00 AM
Gas Stop — Kingman, AZ (~2.5 hrs from Sedona)
  • Kingman, AZ — fill up here before the Mojave stretch. Good last fuel stop on Route 66 before dropping south on I-15 toward Barstow.
Lunch — Barstow or along I-15
  • Idle Spurs Steakhouse, Barstow — reliable stop in the middle of the Mojave, good for a sit-down lunch before the final push into SoCal.
  • In-N-Out Burger, Barstow — if you want something quick and quintessentially Californian on the drive in.
Arrival Redlands, California — approximately 2:30 PM
Destination
  • 76 Lupine Street, Redlands, CA 92373 — end of the road, ~2,100 miles from Tallahassee.

Tips & Reminders

All Hotels Confirmed

All 4 nights are booked and prepaid. La Quinta (Houston, May 13), Quality Inn (El Paso, May 14), Lux Verde (Cottonwood, May 15–17). No further action needed.

Weather in May

Houston ~85°F · El Paso ~88°F · Sedona ~75°F days / 48°F nights. Pack layers for Sedona evenings — canyon temperatures drop fast after sunset.

Gas Strategy

Fill up in Fort Stockton, TX (West Texas is sparse). Also fill in Tucson before Sedona and in Kingman before the Mojave stretch.

Devil's Bridge Parking

Trailhead parking fills by 8 AM on weekends in May. Arrive by 7 AM or take the Uptown Sedona shuttle. A Red Rock Pass ($5/day) is required.

Route Simplicity

You are essentially on I-10 from Tallahassee to Arizona. Straightforward navigation for the first three days — just point west.

Breakfast Credits

Free breakfast at La Quinta (May 14), free breakfast at Quality Inn (May 15), and $20 breakfast credit per day at Lux Verde (May 16 & 17 — use before 11:00 AM checkout).
